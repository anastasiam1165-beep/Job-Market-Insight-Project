## Overview
This project analyzes a comprehensive job market dataset containing 
thousands of job listings across industries, roles, skills, and 
salary ranges. As a Statistics and Analytics student actively 
entering the job market, I chose this dataset to uncover actionable 
insights about in-demand skills, salary benchmarks, and hiring trends 
across data-related roles.

## Objectives
- Identify which roles and industries offer the highest salaries
- Determine the most in-demand skills across job listings
- Analyze how experience level impacts compensation
- Explore hiring trends across industries and locations

## Tools & Technologies
- **Python** — pandas, matplotlib, seaborn
- **Google Colab** — development environment
- **GitHub** — version control and portfolio hosting

## Dataset
- **Source:** Kaggle — Job Market Insight Dataset
- **Contents:** Job titles, required skills, salary ranges, 
  experience levels, industries, and locations
- **Note:** Raw data file not included per Kaggle's 
  redistribution policy

## Key Findings:
- Seattle and San Francisco offer the highest salaries
- Engineering Manager roles command the highest minimum salary at $129k
- Python, AWS, and Git are the top 3 most demanded skills
- Full-time and Contract roles offer similar salary ranges
- Each additional year of experience correlates with ~$X increase in salary
